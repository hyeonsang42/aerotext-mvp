ACN 2075712

Air carrier flight attendant crew reported a thermal runaway from a vape battery charger during boarding. The flight attendant crew notified the Captain, extinguished the fire and placed the battery charger and associated items in the thermal containment bag until the fire fighters arrived and took custody of the bag.
